📍Calves, Hydrotherapy, and Patellofemoral Syndrome

The goals of this topic are:

  • To introduce the physiological effects of the basic massage strokes and techniques (effleurage, petrissage, tapotement, rocking, and cross-fiber friction), plus the therapeutic use of heat, cold, and range-of-motion exercises
  • To begin the four-part hydrotherapy series with an introduction to its principles and applications
  • To introduce the patellofemoral syndrome and the muscles associated with this condition
  • To review lower leg massage techniques used in this session, including adapted elephant walking and calf work coordinated with ankle and foot

After this week, you should be able to:

  • Learning Objectives
    • Identify the specific physiologic effects of effleurage, petrissage, tapotement, rocking, and cross-fiber friction
    • Understand the difference between active and passive range-of-motion exercises and their physiologic effects
    • Have an awareness of the principles of hydrotherapy
    • Understand the anatomy and muscles associated with patellofemoral syndrome

💡 Tip of the Week

Before you begin tapotement (tapping, hacking, cupping, or pounding), let your client know it's coming. Start light at first to introduce, build up to full intensity, try to keep it rhythmic and smooth, then gradually reduce intensity before moving on.
