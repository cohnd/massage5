Welcome to Massage 5!

General Overview of Course

This course explores the discipline and profession of massage therapy. Students will use this Canvas shell for Massage 5 to explore and practice massage techniques, modalities, and protocol development for injury and condition management.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course the student should be able to:

  1. Identify and apply introductory techniques from a range of massage modalities (reflexology, reiki, acupressure, hydrotherapy, and others covered in the term)
  2. Develop a basic massage protocol, including intake questions, for a given injury or condition
  3. Identify the muscles associated with a given injury or condition and adapt technique accordingly
  4. Demonstrate self-care practices (stretches, self-massage, and range-of-motion movements) alongside client-focused technique
  5. Document a massage session using SOAP notes
