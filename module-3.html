📍Chair Massage

The goals of this topic are:

  • To introduce chair massage and fully clothed table massage for events and on-site settings
  • To set up a massage chair safely and comfortably
  • To practice efficient body mechanics
  • To learn a 15-minute chair massage protocol and adapt it to the table

After this week, you should be able to:

  • Describe chair and clothed table massage and how they differ from traditional table massage
  • Adjust a massage chair to fit a client and prevent fainting
  • Demonstrate the eight principles of efficient body use
  • Perform the 15-minute chair massage protocol
  • Give a fully clothed table massage in an event setting
