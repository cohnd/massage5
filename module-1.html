📍Feet, Reflexology, and Plantar Fasciitis

The goals of this topic are:

  • To introduce reflexology as a modality, including its philosophy and core principles
  • To introduce the plantar fasciitis protocol and the muscles of the feet and lower leg associated with this condition
  • To review foot and lower body massage techniques used in this session, including foot massage and ankle/leg traction

After this week, you should be able to:

  • Learning Objectives
    • Have an awareness of the philosophy and principles of reflexology
    • Understand the anatomy and muscles associated with plantar fasciitis
    • Be able to identify the muscles to target when working with the feet
    • Be familiar with foot massage techniques, including compression walking on the feet and traction on the legs holding the ankles and feet
    • Know how to document a reflexology session using SOAP notes
