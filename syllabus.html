🛡️ College Policies

No-Show Drop

Students who do not attend at least one class session during the first week of the term will be administratively dropped through the No-Show Drop process (opens in a new tab). For online courses, attendance is generally defined as participation in at least one required course activity. Refer to your course syllabus for specific requirements for this course.

Student Code of Conduct

Lane Community College students are responsible for knowing the information, policies, and procedures outlined in the Student Code of Conduct (opens in a new tab).

Grade Appeal Policy

Students and faculty, with the support of instructional Deans, should make every effort to resolve concerns about final grades prior to a student seeking a formal grade appeal. Grade appeals to the Grade Appeal Committee (GAC) (opens in a new tab) may be pursued only after such efforts are unsuccessful.

Accessibility and Accommodations

Lane Community College (LCC) is dedicated to providing inclusive learning environments. The Center for Accessible Resources (CAR) coordinates all academic accommodations for students at LCC. If you anticipate or experience academic barriers due to a disability, to request assistance or accommodations, contact the Center for Accessible Resources (541) 463-5150 or accessibleresources@lanecc.edu.

Please be aware that any accessible tables and chairs in this room should remain available for authorized students who find that standard classroom seating is not usable.

Software Privacy Policy Information

Students can find and review privacy policy information of learning software applications used within classes in the Digital Tool Hub (opens in a new tab).
